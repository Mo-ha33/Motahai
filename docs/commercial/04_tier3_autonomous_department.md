2. **Performance Marketing & Media Buying Agencies:**
   - Agencies managing 5 to 20 clients who cannot afford to hire dedicated in-house technical tracking and BI engineers for every account.

### Specialized Capabilities by Swarm Functional Area:
- **Real-Time Network Sniffing (`QS`):** Headless automated browser sessions continuously intercept outgoing HTTP beacons (`facebook.com/tr`, `google-analytics.com/g/collect`, `analytics.tiktok.com`). Instantly detects beacon drops or duplicate hits before ad campaigns waste budget.
- **Deep BI & BigQuery Exports (`BI`):** Connects directly with GA4 raw event exports in BigQuery to unmask hidden checkout drop-offs, compute true blended ROAS, and identify organic vs paid attribution cannibalization.
- **CRO & A/B Testing Blueprints (`CR`):** Analyzes step-by-step cart-to-checkout abandonment and produces statistical hypothesis cards, mockup suggestions, and tag-ready A/B test experiments.
- **Omnichannel Strategy Lead (`GS`):** Synthesizes full funnel data to recommend budget reallocation across Google Search, Shopping, Meta Retargeting, and TikTok Top-of-Funnel.

## 5. Enterprise SLA & Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Protocol

- **Automated Response SLA:** AI Swarm begins investigation within **15 minutes** of any detected tag anomaly or client inquiry.
- **Hermes Autonomous Gatekeeping:** All critical code deployments, GTM container publishing actions, and dataLayer injections pass through Hermes' automated QA verification gate before reaching production.
- **Escalation Protocol:**
  - If a client requests billing changes, high-risk code refactors, or custom contract terms, Lead Orchestrator generates a formal `EscalationNotice` and alerts human staff immediately via Webhook / Slack / Email.
- **Dedicated Human Strategy Touchpoint (Optional Add-On):**
  - Monthly 60-minute Executive Tracking Review with a Senior Technical Partner: **\$350 / session**.
