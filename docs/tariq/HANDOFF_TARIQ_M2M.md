## 2. Decisions applied (user-approved)

## 5. Review notes on the gtm_api.py additions (not blocking)

- `NUM_RETRIES` (default 4) also retries on 403 rateLimit. That's fine for reads and for `workspace_apply` upserts by name, which are idempotent. Keep `num_retries=0` on `create_version` and `publish`.
- Verify-cache (checked): only `CONNECTED` is cached, and client-audience errors invalidate it. Good. Small gap: `apply` and `publish` returning `PERMISSION_MISSING` / `CONTAINER_NOT_FOUND` don't invalidate it, so for up to `GTM_RESOLVE_FRESH_S` (600 s) a re-verify can still say `CONNECTED` after access was revoked. Suggested fix: call `_cache_invalidate(public_id)` on client-audience errors in those paths too.
